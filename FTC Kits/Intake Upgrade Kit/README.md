# Intake Upgrade Kit

The FTC intake attachment with a foam wrap, by Aarav J. (Prolabs Robotics), with R-Factor FRC. The attachment goes on the intake hex shaft, and the foam is wrapped around it until it reaches the POLLEN.

## What is in the kit

| Part | Qty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FTC intake attachment | 1 | 3D print, 100% infill. 180 mm long, 30 mm across, 7 mm hex hole (across flats) all the way through. About 150 g |
| Foam | 1 sheet | Wrapped around the attachment, layer by layer, until it just touches and grips the 2.8 in POLLEN |
| Zip ties | 1 bag | 4 used to hold the foam, the rest are spares |

Tape is not in the kit. Any tape is fine to hold the start of the foam.

## The steps in short

1. Sort the parts and check the print.
2. Slide the attachment onto the intake hex shaft, centre it, and lock it with your shaft collars.
3. Tape the start of the foam along the attachment and make the first wrap, in the same direction the intake spins.
4. Keep wrapping, layer by layer, until the foam reaches the POLLEN. About 50 mm across is a good place to start testing.
5. Check the fit with a real POLLEN. The foam should just touch it and give it a light squeeze (about 2 to 3 mm).
6. Hold the foam with 4 zip ties. Tape the ends if they lift.
7. Spin test, then a final check.

## Not tested yet

- The guide has not been tested on a robot yet. The foam size (about 50 mm across) comes from our [intake calculations](../../FTC%20Intake%20mechanism%20Upgrade/Intake%20Calculations.md) and still needs a check on a real robot. The POLLEN fit check decides the real size.
- Check that your intake hex shaft fits the 7 mm hex hole before you start.
