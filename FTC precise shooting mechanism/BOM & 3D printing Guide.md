## Assembly

A step-by-step visual guide with a 1:1 cutting template is available: [Instruction Guide]()

Quick steps:
1. Print the V2 rail guard (settings below).
2. Cut the flex plate to 11 x 13 holes with scissors.
3. Bend it 19° down after hole 5, then 19° back flat after hole 12.
4. Slide the 5-hole tongue into the slot on the front of V2.
5. Fix V2 and the plate with zip ties, with the tail resting on the floor plate.
6. Roll a ball by hand along the full path before driving.

## 3D Printing

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Material | PETG or PLA+ |
| Infill | **100% (fully solid, no gaps)** |
| Orientation | **Sideways** |
| Supports | **None** |
| Adhesion | 5mm brim |

### Orientation

Print the shooter sideways. Do not print it vertically or in its current orientation.

Why: printing sideways keeps the main shooter curve smooth. A rough curve will cause shooting problems.

### Supports

Do not use any supports while 3D printing.

### Infill

Use 100% infill. A solid part holds its shape and does not crack under ball impact.

## Note

This design has not been tested on a robot yet. Please check the fit by hand before competition use and share feedback with us.
