# Shooter: BOM and 3D Printing Guide

## Bill of Materials

| # | Part | Qty |
|---|------|-----|
| a | 3D printed shooter rail guard (V2) by Aarav Jaisingh, Prolabs Robotics (100% infill) | 1 |
| b | 3D printed Prolabs Flex Sheet: 88 x 104 x 0.2mm, 143 holes (11 x 13, Ø4mm, 8mm grid) | 1 |
| c | Zip ties (2 per rubber band chain) | 8 |
| d | Rubber bands (all the same size) | 4 |
| e | Super glue (optional) | 1 |

### How they connect (2 chains per side, 4 in total)

Sheet hole -> zip tie loop -> rubber band -> zip tie loop -> rail guard hole

## Assembly

A step-by-step visual guide with 3D views is available: [Instruction Guide](https://github.com/Aarav-111/FTC-Bangalore-Resources/blob/main/FTC%20precise%20shooting%20mechanism/V2_flexplate_build_guide.pdf)

Quick steps:
1. Print the V2 rail guard and the Flex Sheet (settings below).
2. Bend the sheet by hand to fit the gap between V2 and the floor plate.
3. Slide one end of the sheet fully into the slot on the front of V2.
4. Fit 2 rubber band chains on each side: sheet hole, zip tie loop, rubber band, zip tie loop, rail guard hole.
5. Keep the zip tie loops loose so the bands can stretch. Trim the zip tie tails so they don't touch the ball.
6. Rest the other end of the sheet on the floor plate.
7. Roll a ball by hand along the full path before driving.

## 3D Printing

| Setting | V2 rail guard | Flex Sheet |
|---|---|---|
| Material | PETG or PLA+ | PETG |
| Infill | **100%** | **100%** |
| Orientation | **Sideways** | **Flat on the bed** |
| Supports | **None** | **None** |
| Layers | Normal | **One 0.2mm layer** |
| Adhesion | 5mm brim | Level bed, slow first layer |

### Orientation

Print the rail guard sideways. Do not print it vertically or in its current orientation.

Why: printing sideways keeps the main shooter curve smooth. A rough curve will cause shooting problems.

### Supports

Do not use any supports for either part.

### Infill

Use 100% infill for both parts.

## Note

This design has not been tested on a robot yet. The 0.2mm sheet is very thin and can sag under the ball. Please check the fit by hand before competition use and share feedback with us.
