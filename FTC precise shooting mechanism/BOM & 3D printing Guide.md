# Shooter: BOM and 3D Printing Guide

## Bill of Materials

| # | Part | Qty |
|---|------|-----|
| a | 3D printed shooter rail guard (V2) by Aarav Jaisingh, Prolabs Robotics (100% infill) | 1 |
| b | Flex plate: 11 x 13 holes (88 x 104mm), cut from goBILDA Medium Polycarbonate Gridplate 1117-0088-0352 (one plate makes 3) | 1 |
| c | Zip ties (2 per rubber band chain) | 8 |
| d | Rubber bands (all the same size) | 4 |
| e | Super glue (optional) | 1 |

### How they connect (2 chains per side, 4 in total)

Plate hole -> zip tie loop -> rubber band -> zip tie loop -> rail guard hole

## Assembly

A step-by-step visual guide with a 1:1 cutting template is available: [Instruction Guide]()

Quick steps:
1. Print the V2 rail guard (settings below).
2. Cut the flex plate to 11 x 13 holes with scissors.
3. Bend it 19° down after hole 5, then 19° back flat after hole 12.
4. Slide the 5-hole tongue fully into the slot on the front of V2.
5. Fit 2 rubber band chains on each side: plate hole, zip tie loop, rubber band, zip tie loop, rail guard hole.
6. Keep the zip tie loops loose so the bands can stretch. Trim the zip tie tails so they don't touch the ball.
7. Rest the plate tail on the floor plate.
8. Pull the plate gently away from V2. It should spring back fully into the slot.
9. Roll a ball by hand along the full path before driving.

## 3D Printing

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Material | PETG or PLA+ |
| Infill | **100% (fully solid, no gaps)** |
| Orientation | **Sideways** |
| Supports | **None** |
| Adhesion | 5mm brim |

### Orientation

Print the shooter sideways. Do not print it vertically or in its current orientation.

Why: printing sideways keeps the main shooter curve smooth. A rough curve will cause shooting problems.

### Supports

Do not use any supports while 3D printing.

### Infill

Use 100% infill. A solid part holds its shape and does not crack when the ball hits it.

## Note

This design has not been tested on a robot yet. Please check the fit by hand before competition use and share feedback with us.
