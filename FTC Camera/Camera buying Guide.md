## 4. What do the specifications mean?

| Feature | Meaning | When it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 720p | 1280 × 720 pixels | Basic calls when cost matters most |
| 1080p | 1920 × 1080 pixels | Sharper calls and recordings |
| 4K | 3840 × 2160 pixels | Cropping, detailed recording, or compatible high-resolution workflows |
| 30 FPS | Up to 30 frames per second | Standard video calls and recordings |
| Autofocus | Automatically adjusts focus as the subject or distance changes | Showing robotics parts or objects close to the camera |
| Fixed focus | Focus is set for a particular range | Mostly stationary use |
| Field of view (FOV) | How much of the scene the camera captures | Showing a desk or more than one person |
| Light correction | Adjusts the image in dim conditions | Rooms without strong lighting |
| Privacy shutter | Physically covers the lens | Preventing accidental camera exposure |

A higher resolution does not automatically mean better video. Sensor quality, lens quality, lighting, focus, and software also matter.

## 5. Buying checklist

- [ ] Confirm the maximum resolution and frame rate.
- [ ] Check for autofocus if you will show objects close to the lens.
- [ ] Confirm compatibility with your computer and video software.
- [ ] Check USB-A/USB-C compatibility and whether an adapter is needed.
- [ ] Compare warranty and return policies.
- [ ] Read recent reviews about image and microphone quality.
- [ ] Improve lighting: a lamp in front of you can noticeably improve the image.
